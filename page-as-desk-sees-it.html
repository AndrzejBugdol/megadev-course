#4471 · two problems, not one

owner support desk · closed · reviewed 2026-11-26

#4471 merged 412 reports about wrong dates and marked them known, with one fix queued. Read one by one, the reports hold two problems. One fix does not close both.

Not one issue. Two tickets, two fixes. The parser fix covers the first problem only.

The two problems

#ProblemReports
1parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get day and month swapped.379
2hint: tenants on the booking-v3 form are told to type MM/DD/YYYY, and the form then reads it day first.33

379 + 33 = 412. Five of the 379 parser reports read as a one-day move (a time zone or day-boundary effect); the desk counts them under parser and footnotes them.

How to tell them apart

  1. Locale. Ask the platform whether the tenant's locale writes the day first.
  2. Form. A day-first tenant reporting through booking-v3's form is hint. Any other day-first report is parser.
  3. Shape. A day and month swap is problem 1 or 2.

Intake's intakeTag is a guess from the reporter's browser: 15 of the 33 hint reports were tagged parser.

Outcome

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October.

  • parser: fixed, fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint: won't fix, the booking-v3 form is retired.

Why it was merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. Nobody read the bodies, so two bugs became one ticket. A new decision record should replace 0001 for date reports.

What to do

  • Check the five one-day-move reports against the parser fix.
  • Tell the 33 hint reporters that #4471 does not cover their bug.

Lessons

  • Merge by cause, not by subject. Count the reports under each cause.
  • A "known" mark on a merged ticket says nothing about the causes inside it.
