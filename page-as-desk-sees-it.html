#4471 · three problems, not one

owner support desk · known · reviewed 2026-11-26

#4471 merged 412 reports about wrong dates and marked them known, with one fix queued. Read one by one, the reports hold three problems. One fix does not close all three.

Not one issue. Three tickets, three fixes. The queued parser fix covers the first problem only.

The three problems

#ProblemReports
1parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get day and month swapped.374
2hint: tenants on the booking-v3 form are told to type MM/DD/YYYY, and the form then reads it day first.33
3one-day move: a booking shifts by exactly one day. A time zone or day-boundary bug, not a month flip.5

374 + 33 + 5 = 412.

How to tell them apart

  1. Locale. Ask the platform whether the tenant's locale writes the day first.
  2. Form. A day-first tenant reporting through booking-v3's form is hint. Any other day-first report is parser.
  3. Shape. A day and month swap is problem 1 or 2. A move of exactly one day is problem 3.

Intake's intakeTag is a guess from the reporter's browser: 15 of the 33 hint reports were tagged parser.

Why it was merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. Nobody read the bodies, so three bugs became one ticket. A new decision record should replace 0001 for date reports.

What to do

  • Keep the parser fix queued, scoped to problem 1.
  • Give problem 2 its own fix: change the booking-v3 hint, or make the form read what the hint says.
  • Open an investigation for problem 3.
  • Tell the 33 and 5 reporters that #4471 does not cover their bug.

Lessons

  • Merge by cause, not by subject. Count the reports under each cause.
  • A "known" mark on a merged ticket says nothing about the causes inside it.
